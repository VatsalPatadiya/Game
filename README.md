# Mahjong Sanctuary

A premium mahjong solitaire game for mobile, built around a calm, elegant jade-and-gold aesthetic.

## Gameplay

- **Tray-collection matching** — tap a free tile to send it to a 4-slot tray. Two identical tiles collected in the tray clear together. Filling the tray with four non-matching tiles ends the round.
- **Classic pyramid board** — tiles are arranged in a stacked "turtle" pyramid; only tiles that aren't covered and have a clear side are tappable at any moment.
- **Endless levels** — 5 hand-crafted starter levels, then an unlimited stream of procedurally generated levels after that. The game never runs out of content.
- **Difficulty that keeps growing** — later levels get harder through more look-alike tile symbols, tighter windows of available matches, and (from level ~4 difficulty onward) a portion of tiles that start face-down and must be revealed with a tap before they can be collected.
- **Hidden tile reveal** — tapping a face-down tile flips it face-up so you can see its value before deciding to collect it. Only one tile can be "peeked" at a time; revealing a different one flips the first back down.
- **Combo streaks** — matching tiles in quick succession builds a combo counter with its own celebration effect.

## Player aids

- **Hint** — highlights a valid pair without spending it for you.
- **Undo** — returns the most recently collected tile back to the board, exactly as it was (including re-hiding it if it was a concealed tile).
- **Shuffle** — reshuffles the values of the remaining tiles on the board when you're stuck.

Each aid has a limited number of uses per level, and using fewer of them earns a higher star rating (up to 3 stars) when you clear the board.

## Progression

- Levels unlock one at a time as you win them.
- Your best star rating on every level is saved, along with which levels you've unlocked — progress survives closing and reopening the app.
- Sound and vibration preferences are saved separately and take effect immediately.

## Presentation

- Fully 3D tile board with a soft tilt, bevelled tile edges, drop shadows, and smooth deal-in/collection animations.
- Premium jade, gold, and ivory visual theme with classical Cinzel display type throughout menus and headings.
- Branded app icon and launch screen featuring the game's own seal mark (an arched-gate motif echoing the level-start screen's own door).
- Level-start screen shows the level number and a difficulty label (Easy / Medium / Hard) before you begin.
- Pause menu with sound and vibration toggles.
- Win/lose screen showing your score and stars earned.

## Analytics

The game tracks key player actions (starting and finishing levels, matches, combos, hint/undo/shuffle usage, and hidden-tile reveals) to help understand how people actually play and where they get stuck, so future levels and difficulty pacing can be tuned around real behavior.
